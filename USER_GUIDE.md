# CareerPath AI User Guide

CareerPath AI helps you explore education and career choices at your own pace.
You can ask a question, discuss your interests, or explore courses before
sharing grades. Grades are optional.

## 1. Create an account or sign in

1. Open the CareerPath AI site and choose **Register here** if you are new.
2. Enter your name, email address, and password.
3. Check your email and enter the verification code, or use the verification
   link if one is provided.
4. Sign in with your username or email and password. Google sign-in is also
   available.

If you forget your password, select **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in page
and follow the emailed instructions.

## 2. Start a conversation

1. Select **New chat** in the sidebar.
2. Type a question or describe what you are interested in. You can ask about
   careers, courses, institutions, or study options.
3. Select the send button. While the assistant is preparing a response, the
   send control shows that it is thinking.
4. Continue by typing a follow-up or choosing one of the optional suggestions.

You can ask more than one question in a message. If you are unsure where to
begin, ask to explore your interests. The assistant can guide you through a
short, optional discovery conversation.

## 3. Explore courses and recommendations

You do not need to enter grades to discuss careers or learn about courses.
When you ask for personalized course recommendations, the app may ask for
grades or study preferences to help assess entry requirements.

- Share only the grades you want considered.
- You can leave grades out and still explore course options.
- If a relevant grade is missing, eligibility cannot be confirmed for that
  requirement.
- Treat admission requirements as a starting point and confirm current details
  with the institution or official admissions source.

Use **Profile Setup** to provide information for recommendations. The
**Intelligence Report** section presents generated guidance and **Saved
Reports** provides access to saved reports.

## 4. Attach a file to chat

Use the attachment button to add reference material to a message. Supported
formats are PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP, MP4, MOV, and WebM. You can attach up to
three files at a time; each file must be 5 MB or smaller.

Only attach material you are comfortable sharing with the service. Check
important details extracted from a file and correct them if needed.

## 5. Find previous conversations

Conversations are saved in the browser on the device where you used the app.
Open **Previous chats** in the sidebar to select a saved conversation. This
browser storage is not a substitute for a backup and may not be available on
another device or browser.

On a desktop, use the sidebar control to hide or show the sidebar. On a
mobile-sized screen, use the navigation menu button to open it; tap outside
the menu or press Escape to close it.

## 6. Account settings and reports

Open **Account Settings** to review available profile and account actions,
including changing your account details or deleting your account. Account
deletion is permanent; make sure you want to proceed before confirming.

Generated reports can be reviewed and saved from the report-related sections
of the dashboard. If an email or report action is unavailable, try again
later or contact the service administrator.

## Privacy and good practice

- Do not share passwords, verification codes, or other credentials in chat.
- Share only the personal information and grades needed for your question.
- Verify course requirements, costs, and admissions decisions with official
  sources.
- AI responses can be incomplete or out of date; use them as guidance, not as
  a guarantee of admission or a substitute for official advice.
