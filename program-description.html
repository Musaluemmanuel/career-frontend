CareerPath AI

An intelligent career companion for Kenyan students. CareerPath AI combines conversational guidance, course discovery, grade-aware recommendations, document analysis, and saved reports to help learners make clearer decisions about education and career pathways.

How it helps

  • Explores interests through friendly, scenario-based conversations.
  • Explains courses, institutions, admission requirements, and study options.
  • Provides personalised recommendations when grades or preferences are available.
  • Reads supported documents, images, and videos to assist explanations.
  • Saves conversations and produces useful reports without losing the student's context.
