# Community Code of Conduct

We aim to make collaboration around CareerPath AI respectful, constructive,
and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Focus criticism on the work, explain concerns clearly, and welcome
  good-faith questions.
- Share useful context and help keep discussions accessible and on topic.
- Protect students' privacy; do not post personal data, credentials, or
  private conversation content.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Reporting and response

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer. Do not include
student data or secrets in a report. Maintainers should review concerns
promptly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and take proportionate
action, including removing content or restricting participation when needed.
